# Half-Life icon provenance

`Rx/HalfLife.icon/icon.json` is structurally adapted from Apple's AppIcon.icon sample in Configuring Your App to Use Alternate App Icons:
https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/configuring-your-app-to-use-alternate-app-icons
https://docs-assets.developer.apple.com/published/1bbbd245213e/ConfiguringYourAppToUseAlternateAppIcons.zip

Gradient values, groups, and image references were modified. The transparent Half-Life image layers are original project artwork; no Apple sample artwork was copied. Apple's sample-code copyright and permission notice is retained in `LICENSES/Apple-Sample-Code.txt`. No license for the entire app is implied by that third-party notice.
